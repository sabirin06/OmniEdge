# OmniEdge branding register

Verified 24 November 2026 against the [official OmniTeckno homepage](https://www.omniteckno.com/).

- The header and footer use [logo_dark.png](https://www.omniteckno.com/static/images/logo_dark.png). OmniEdge bundles the original PNG and displays it on sign-in and in the sidebar.
- The [published stylesheet](https://www.omniteckno.com/static/css/main.css) identifies cyan `#00BFFF` and royal blue `#0047B8` as primary brand colors. OmniEdge uses cyan for accents and royal blue for primary actions with white button text.
- The shared theme uses the published dark canvas `#060B14`, surface `#0B1322`, borders `#1A2844`, text `#F8FAFC`, and secondary text `#94A3B8`. Inter is the site's body typeface and is used in the application.
- Status and severity badges retain explicit text labels.

These values and the logo are verified against public company assets. Separate approval of an internal brand guide has not been obtained.
